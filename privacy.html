Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Who we are

Jerpen Social Planner is a personal tool run locally on the owner's computer. Contact: via the GitHub profile github.com/xJerpen

2. What the tool processes

To post on your behalf, the tool receives from the connected platform (YouTube/Google, Instagram, TikTok): an access token, your account or channel name, and basic account information needed to show which account is connected. It also processes the video files and texts that you choose to post.

3. Where data is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the computer where the tool runs. Video files are kept temporarily on that computer while posting and are deleted afterwards. We do not run a server that collects your data, we do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or analytics.

4. Data sent to platforms

Content you post is sent to the platform you selected (for example TikTok) and is then handled under that platform's own privacy policy.

5. Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The YouTube access is used only to upload videos you choose and to show the name of the connected channel.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect an account in the tool, which deletes its stored token. You can also revoke access in your Google, Instagram or TikTok account settings. To ask about or request deletion of any data, contact via the GitHub profile github.com/xJerpen.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
