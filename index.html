Jerpen Social Planner

Jerpen Social Planner is a personal tool, run locally on the owner's own computer, that lets the owner of the Jerpen channel (sim racing and car content) upload their own videos to their own YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Discord accounts from one place.

The tool is not a public service. It is only used by its owner and, on request, by clients who give explicit permission to post to their own accounts.
